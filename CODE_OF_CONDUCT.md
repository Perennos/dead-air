# Community conduct

This project monitors streams; it does not grant permission to inspect, record, redistribute, or interfere with them. Keep that boundary clear in discussion and code review.

## Expected conduct

- Be direct, patient, and respectful. Critique behavior or code without targeting a person.
- Respect stream owners, performers, viewers, and the communities represented by a stream. Do not post private URLs, credentials, personal details, or unredacted traces.
- Ask before using someone's stream, recording, image, name, or story in a report, demo, or example. A technically reachable source is not consent.
- For Día de Muertos remembrance or cultural collaboration, follow the direction and consent of the people involved. Treat it as community remembrance and collaboration, not Halloween framing or a decorative theme.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, dox, or use project spaces to coordinate unauthorized access or disruption.

## Reporting and response

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers using the contact route provided by the repository owner. Do not include stream credentials or private configurations in the report. Maintainers may remove unsafe material, pause a discussion, or restrict participation while they review a concern. They should share only the information needed to address it and avoid promising confidentiality they cannot ensure.

For a vulnerability, use the private reporting route in `SECURITY.md`. This project is not an emergency or incident-response service.
